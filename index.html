Architecture

Three Zoho apps, one flow

Creator is the system of record for bookings. Every booking appears in CRM for sales, and every completed stay is invoiced in Books.

Zoho CRM

  • Account + Contact per guest

Deluge business rules

Server-side validation: overlap detection on half-open date ranges, a 365-day limit, and a status lifecycle that locks final records.

rental_validate.dg →

CRM integration

Booking → Deal, with Account and Contact matched by name and email. The Deal ID is stored on the booking, so saving again updates the same Deal.

crm_sync.dg →

CRM development

A workflow rule and a Deluge function call the Creator API. They cancel only reserved bookings and can't loop. The connection uses minimal OAuth scopes.

deal_closed_lost.dg →

Books invoicing

A completed stay creates a draft invoice: guest, nights × rate, rental reference. The total matches the dashboard's realized revenue.
